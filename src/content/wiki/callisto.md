Callisto is the outermost of Jupiter’s four great moons, and the only one where people from the inner system can live without heavy shielding. It lies outside Jupiter’s main radiation belts. Its surface dose is about 0.01 rem a day, lower than in low Earth orbit. That one fact made it the capital of the [United Solar Nations](/wiki/united-solar-nations/) at Jupiter.

## The Green Zone

Under the [Jovian Leasehold](/wiki/jovian-leasehold/), Callisto is the **Green Zone**: administration, finance, trade, and the polite face of civilization. Its geology is old, cold and stable, which makes it the right place for the one industry the USN guards above all others: the **refineries**.

Raw gas scooped from Jupiter is mostly hydrogen and ordinary helium. Separating out the helium-3 means chilling it to within two degrees of absolute zero. There, ordinary helium becomes a superfluid that flows through filters helium-3 can’t pass. The process is sensitive to vibration and needs a steady foundation, which Callisto provides. Every scoop crew in the system has to bring its catch here. That makes Callisto the chokepoint of the whole fuel economy. See [Helium-3](/wiki/helium-3/).

Callisto is also on the rim of Jupiter’s gravity well, the cheapest place to launch refined fuel back toward the inner system.

## Skuld City

Built into a ninety-kilometer impact crater, **Skuld City** is a ring that turns inside a **cowl**: a fixed shell of insulation built into the crater wall that the ring never touches. The ring spins to give eight-tenths of a gee. Callisto has gravity of its own, though, so “down” on the ring tips nine degrees off the floor. Water falling from a tap curves. Newcomers walk the ring in a lean. Most need three or four days before they stop leaning the wrong way.

- A pod ride from the hub at the spin axis down to the rim takes about ninety seconds.
- The **Skuld Maglev** runs in the vacuum gap between ring and cowl.
- Radiator curtains stand all around the crater rim, droplet sheets falling through them. See [Heat](/wiki/heat/).
- The **Jovian Claim Registry** is here. It is the only place a helium-3 leasehold can be finalized, and only in person.
- The deep-space arrays stand on the static rim, out on dead rock.

In orbit, Callisto Logistics Node One handles the moon’s heavy traffic. Everyone calls it **Hell One**.


<aside class="science">

## Behind the science

Callisto is real, and its numbers hold. It orbits about 1.88 million kilometers from Jupiter, and its ancient surface is the most heavily cratered in the solar system. Skuld is a real crater on Callisto, about 92 kilometers across, named in 1979 for the Norn of Norse myth who governs fate. The city inside it is invented.

The refining method is real physics too. Below about 2.17 kelvin, helium-4 becomes a superfluid with no viscosity. It can creep through pores far too fine for anything else, including helium-3, which stays an ordinary liquid at that temperature. Engineers call a filter like this a “superleak.”

Skuld City’s tilted floor is what you get when you spin a ring on a moon that already has gravity. The ring’s spin and the moon’s pull add together, and the floor is set square to the spin.
