Out here a name has to work over a bad radio, in a hurry, from someone breathing hard. So the names people actually use are short and hard-edged, and they carry a lot of history.

## The polite names

| Group | Formal | What people say |
|---|---|---|
| Inner system | United Solar Nations | the USN, **the Core**, Geneva |
| Belt and outer system | Ceres Protection Agreement | **Capa** |
| Mars | Tharsis Republic | Tharsis |
| Saturn | Titan Technate Collective | the Technate |

**Jupiter or Jovian?** Working people say *Jupiter*: every hull at Jupiter, the busiest port at Jupiter. Offices and law say *Jovian*: the Jovian Leasehold, the Jovian Claim Registry, the Jovian theater. One word, two classes.

**Thieves or pirates?** Someone who takes a claim before it’s legally owned is a **thief** to the registry. A hull that has strayed off its logged route is a **pirate** to a warship, and a warship doesn’t need a warrant for a pirate.

## The rude names

The inner system has names for the Capa that make fun of their light, low-gravity bodies and their home:

- **Low**, **Lows**: low gravity, low value, low life.
- **Hollow**, **Hollows**: the empty Belt, the hollow rocks they live in. *The Hollows* can mean the Belt itself.
- **Rock-rat**, **Void-rat**.

The Capa answer in kind, mostly about gravity and wet planets:

- **Dirt**, **dirt-born**: born on a planet. Heavy-boned, heavy-footed.
- **Well**, **Wells**: from the gravity well they live at the bottom of.
- **Wet**: soft, water-bodied, planet-raised.
- **Grounder**, **Heavy**.
- **Blues**: inner-system navy and security, after the uniforms.

Titan gets its own kind of name. It isn’t an insult so much as unease: **Null** and **Void**, for a people whose ships and minds nobody can read.


<aside class="science">

## Behind the science

Many of these names had to pass a radio test. Under stress, speech gets faster and louder. Soft hissing sounds (*s*, *f*, *sh*) are the first to drown in static. Voice-activated mics also clip the start of a word.

So names built on hard stops survive. *Capa*, *Low*, *Dirt*, *Core* and *Null* all pass. Longer insults, like the ones about tall, stick-thin Belt bodies, mostly fail the test. They get shortened, or they fall out of use.

</aside>
