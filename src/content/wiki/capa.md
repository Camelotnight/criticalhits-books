**Capa** is what the people of the Belt and the outer system call themselves. The word grew out of the initials of their alliance, the **CPA**, the Ceres Protection Agreement. Said quickly over a radio, C-P-A became *Capa*.

You will meet three words for the same people:

- **Capa** is the people and their things: *Capa crews*, *a Capa vessel*, *Capa caf*. This is the word you will see most.
- **CPA** is the official side: the alliance and its law, as in *CPA registry* or *CPA Maritime Code*.
- **The Belt Code** is custom: the working rules the Capa live by, whatever the law books say. See [Search and rescue](/wiki/search-and-rescue/).

## Bodies built for out here

Capa are born and raised in low gravity, on lower spin than the big stations give anyone. Many grow tall and slender, with long limbs and a rolling, easy gait in light gravity. Many also carry voluntary adaptations: radiation-hardened skin and implants that help the inner ear keep balance through spin and hard burns. See [Augmentation](/wiki/augmentation/).

Those bodies are the reason the Capa matter. Jupiter’s inner moons and atmosphere are bathed in radiation that wears baseline people down fast. Capa crews can work there. Inner-system crews mostly can’t. Under the [Jovian Leasehold](/wiki/jovian-leasehold/), that **Adaptation Gap** gives the Capa their one real piece of leverage: the USN owns the fuel, but the Capa own the hands that dig it out.

## Where they live

About 95 million Capa live in more than 2,400 habitats across the Belt and the outer system. Their home ground is [the Belt](/wiki/the-belt/). At Jupiter, most of them gather at the trailing Trojan camp, L5, around a rock called Patroclus. See [The Trojans](/wiki/the-trojans/).

## Money and habits

Helium-3 is the Capa’s single largest expense, and they buy it from the people who refine it. So Capa ships coast whenever they can and burn only for corrections. The Belt Code expects every ship to keep an emergency fuel reserve for rescues, and that rule has grown into a habit of hoarding. Capa crews take the jobs that pay a [hazard premium](/wiki/glossary/#hazard-premium), and they wait on lists for places they built. As one Capa says of [Hercules](/wiki/hercules/): *“Built the wall and can’t get through it.”*


<aside class="science">

## Behind the science

Why *Capa* and not C-P-A? Because the short form survives a bad radio link. In static, soft sounds like *s*, *f* and *sh* are the first to vanish. Voice-activated mics also tend to clip the first syllable of a word. Hard “plosive” consonants like *k* and *p* trigger the mic cleanly and stay readable through noise.

*Capa* is two syllables with two plosives. It works when shouted by someone out of breath. A name full of hissing letters would not.

</aside>
