Jupiter doesn’t travel alone. Sixty degrees ahead of it, and sixty degrees behind, the Sun’s pull and Jupiter’s balance out. Asteroids have collected at those two points since the solar system formed. Together they are called the **Trojans**, and they are the doorways to Jupiter.

## L4: the front door

The leading camp, also called the **Greek camp**, is where traffic from the inner system arrives. It is the more crowded of the two swarms, which means more rock to build with and more places to put guns. The USN holds it.

- **Minerva Station**, the USN’s anchor at Jupiter. See [Minerva Station](/wiki/minerva-station/).
- **Hercules**, the habitat cylinders under construction. See [Hercules](/wiki/hercules/).
- **Achilles**, the USN’s customs complex and parts depot, where certified parts are sold at certified prices.
- **Hektor**, the Titan Technate’s depot, where nitrogen tankers deliver the air of L4. See [Titan Technate Collective](/wiki/titan-technate/).

## L5: the back door

The trailing camp, the **Trojan camp**, is the natural departure point for Saturn and the outer system. It is also the back door into the Belt for anyone who would rather not pass USN customs. The USN keeps a nominal presence. The Capa run it.

- **Patroclus** is the hub of the spacer population at Jupiter: docks, an administrative block and a chartered terminal.
- Capa habitats are built as **distributed clusters** spread through the swarm, not one big station.
- Independent repair yards keep the aging Belt fleet flying with work the USN calls non-compliant.
- Religious breakaway communities and wandering fleets live out here too. They often act as neutral ground between USN and Capa.

Beyond L5 lie **the Wilds**: empty, lawless space where a ship can go to disappear. Spacers call the open volume beyond the lanes and the law the **deep void**.

## What the rock is made of

Most Trojans are dark, primitive **D-type** and **P-type** asteroids. They are rich in carbon and organics, with a little nitrogen locked in. That makes them a natural quarry for building habitats.


<aside class="science">

## Behind the science

The names come from a real tradition. Astronomers name the L4 Trojans after Greek heroes of the Trojan War and the L5 Trojans after Trojan ones. Two early discoveries broke the rule before it was set, and they stayed in the “wrong” camps. **624 Hektor**, a Trojan hero, sits among the Greeks at L4. It is the largest Jupiter Trojan. **617 Patroclus**, a Greek, sits among the Trojans at L5.

Patroclus is actually a pair, Patroclus and Menoetius, two bodies orbiting each other. NASA’s Lucy mission is scheduled to visit them. The book’s geography is built around those real rocks.

</aside>
