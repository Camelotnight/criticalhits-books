**Cooper’s Reach**, the *Reach* to her crew, is a *Lifeline*-class heavy rescue cutter on a civilian search-and-rescue charter. Captain Kade Holloway inherited her from his father, Jack. She carries Ceres registry.

She is overbuilt for her size. Her fusion torch is more engine than a cutter should carry. The torch gave the ship options, and options kept people alive. That same oversized torch is too non-standard to clear certification easily. Like her captain, she is good at her job and always one part away from trouble.

## The first rule

*My air is your air.* It is the first rule on Kade’s ship.

## Aboard

- A **spin ring** sixteen meters across at six-tenths of a gee holds the galley, the crew’s quarters and a four-bed medbay. Its worn bearing chirps once every 7.2 seconds. See [Spin gravity](/wiki/spin-gravity/).
- The **command deck** sits outside the ring, unspun.
- **The Pelt**, her layered armor, catches debris before it reaches the hull.
- **The Curtain**, her droplet radiators, deploys in the shadow of her tapered stern. See [Heat](/wiki/heat/).
- **Caretaker**, the ship’s mind, lives in a crash-rated black box on the keel. See [Caretaker](/wiki/caretaker/).
- Rescue kit: a bow plasma lance, three boarding umbilicals called “the lifelines,” crawler drones and a debris-clearing laser. No offensive weapons.

## Life aboard

Her water is recycled to the last drop: chemically pure, flat, faintly metallic. Her galley grows a wall of micro-greens and runs a synthesizer that extrudes standard Capa nutritional paste from algae and yeast. Her crew drinks Capa caf from bulbs, and calls it an acquired taste.
