The United Solar Nations is the oldest and richest power in the solar system. Its capital is Geneva, on Earth, and its industrial heart is the Moon. People out at Jupiter call it the USN, or simply **the Core**. When it acts officially, the word on the paperwork is **Mandate**: Mandate custody, Mandate transit lanes, a Mandate investigator.

## What it holds

The USN funded the first infrastructure in the outer system: the mass drivers, the orbital refineries, the relays. It also holds the patents on the fusion reactors that create the demand for [helium-3](/wiki/helium-3/). On those two facts it claims **sovereign title** to the fuel of the whole Jovian system. That claim is the backbone of the [Jovian Leasehold](/wiki/jovian-leasehold/).

In practice the USN controls three things:

- **The refineries.** Every ton of raw gas scooped from Jupiter has to be refined on [Callisto](/wiki/callisto/), and the USN owns every refinery there.
- **The money.** The system’s currency, the [shu](/wiki/shu/), is a USN standard.
- **The stamp.** Sovereign Vendor Certification decides which parts, ships and contractors may work USN jobs. An uncertified part can be just as good. It still can’t fly a USN contract.

## Its weakness

Earth’s 12.8 billion people run on helium-3 fusion, and the USN buys almost all of the system’s supply. It cannot afford an interruption of even a few weeks. But its people are built for a full gee and a thick atmosphere. Jupiter’s radiation and long low-gravity tours wear them down. So the USN owns the mine and cannot work it. The [Capa](/wiki/capa/) can. That gap shapes everything at Jupiter.

## How it looks out here

- Its warships carry the prefix **USNS**, United Solar Nations Ship, the way a navy hull carries USS or HMS.
- At Jupiter it governs from Callisto and from [Minerva Station](/wiki/minerva-station/), where the Outer Territories Development Authority, known as **the Directorate**, runs the Leasehold.
- USN settlers clear the [Hercules](/wiki/hercules/) waiting list first.

## The inner system

Behind the USN’s reach at Jupiter stands an industrial machine that rebuilt the inner planets: the deep warrens of Luna, Mercury’s solar arrays and the floating refineries of Venus. See [The inner system](/wiki/inner-system/).


<aside class="science">

## Behind the science

Why can’t Earth just supply everything itself? Gravity. To throw a ton of anything off Earth you have to reach about 11.2 km/s, and most of the fuel you burn is spent lifting fuel. Mars and the Belt sit in far shallower wells. Shipping *down* toward the Sun is cheap; lifting *up* out of Earth is not.

So the USN’s strategy is built on that physics. It keeps its people where the gravity is deep and the population is huge. Then it controls what flows *into* the well, the fuel, the standards and the money, rather than trying to haul industry out of it.

</aside>
